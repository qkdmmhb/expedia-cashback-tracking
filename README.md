## What Expedia packages actually are — and what "save up to $974" means

An Expedia package is any combination of flight + hotel (optionally + car rental) booked in a single transaction. Expedia's package deals page promotes savings "up to $974" for flight + hotel bundles, and the mechanics are straightforward: you enter a destination and dates, pick from pre-built offers or assemble your own, and pay once instead of three times.

The headline number needs context, though. Expedia's own terms state that savings vary and aren't available on all packages, and the comparison is against the price of the same components booked separately **before** any discounts or rewards. Actual savings depend on your origin, destination, trip length, and travel dates. So $974 is a ceiling, not a promise — but even half of that on a family trip is real money.

A few package features worth knowing before you book:

- **All-inclusive filter.** Select it and you'll see resorts where food, drinks, and activities are built into the price. Conditions vary by property, so check each one.
- **Free cancellation** is available on many package bookings, plus buy-now-pay-later options at checkout.
- **Member prices.** Signing into a free Expedia account unlocks member pricing, and One Key members can get up to 30% off when combining a hotel with a flight.
- **Per-person pricing quirks.** Package prices are quoted per person, based on twin share of the lowest-priced room type and lowest available airfare, inclusive of hotel taxes. Some airfares in packages are nonrefundable and nontransferable, and baggage fees may not be included — worth reading before you commit.

## The fine print that decides whether you get paid

Most "my cashback didn't track" complaints come down to a handful of documented rules. These are the ones that matter for an Expedia package:

- **Cashback is calculated on the item price only**, excluding taxes, shipping, and other fees. On a package, that means your rebate base is smaller than the checkout total.
- **Unlisted coupon codes kill tracking.** Using a code you found somewhere other than TopCashback's own page, paying partially or fully with gift cards, or stacking conflicting offers can void the rebate.
- **Browser matters.** Turn off ad blockers and pop-up blockers, don't browse incognito, and don't switch devices mid-booking. TopCashback specifically warns that **Safari can't reliably track cashback** — use Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
